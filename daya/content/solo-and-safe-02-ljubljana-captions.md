## Instagram caption

Ljubljana might be the easiest first solo trip in Europe. 💚

Slovenia is one of the safest countries in the world, and its small capital feels made for your first trip alone. The old town has no cars - just the river, a few bridges, and quiet cafes you can walk between all day. Crime is low, everything is close, and most people speak English.

5 reasons to start here:
1. Low crime, and very easy to walk everywhere
2. A car-free old town - calm, not stressful
3. Take the cable car up to the castle for the best view over the city and the Alps
4. Ride the free "Kavalir" cart that locals use to cross the old town
5. Lake Bled is only 40 minutes away by bus - go and come back the same day

Save this for your first solo trip, and send it to a friend who travels alone. 💚
