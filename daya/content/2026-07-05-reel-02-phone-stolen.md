## Die Timeline (Slides 2-7 / Szenen)

**Minute 0-5 - Lock it.**
Borrow any phone or open any browser. Find My (Apple) or Find My Device
(Google) - "Mark as Lost". Your phone locks itself and shows a message.

**Minute 5-10 - Block your cards.**
Banking app on any device. From Germany abroad: +49 116 116 blocks
every German card, 24/7, one call.

**Minute 10-15 - Change your email password.**
Email first, always. It is the master key to everything else you own.

**Minute 15-25 - Police report.**
Ask for the case number in writing. Your insurance pays with it - not without.

**Minute 25-30 - Text your person.**
From any device: "Phone stolen. I am safe. Reach me here." Then breathe.
