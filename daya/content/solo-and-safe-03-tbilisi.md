## Caption (full text, hyphens only, ends with save + send + 💚)
> The safe little capital nobody books first.
>
> Georgia barely makes the average solo-travel list, and that is exactly why it is such a
> gentle first trip. Crime is low, prices are tiny, and hospitality is almost a religion here:
> "the guest is a gift" is a real rule, not a slogan. Tbilisi's old town is small and walkable,
> all cobblestones, carved balconies and sulfur baths.
>
> What to do:
> 1. Ride the cable car up to Narikala Fortress for the whole city under your feet
> 2. Skip taxis, the metro and marshrutka cost cents
> 3. Day-trip to Mtskheta, 30 minutes away: ancient churches, the old capital, back by lunch
>
> Save this for your first solo trip, and send it to the friend who keeps saying "one day". 💚
