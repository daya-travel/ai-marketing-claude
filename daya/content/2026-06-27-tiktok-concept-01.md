# TikTok - Concept - "green flags a city is safe to start solo" (faceless voiceover)

**Account:** @her.solotrip (TikTok)
**Goal:** reach via completion + search (keywords)
**Format:** TikTok, 12–18 sec, 9:16, **faceless** voiceover over b-roll (no talking-to-camera)
**Theme:** safety
**Language:** English.
**Important:** Same idea as the IG Reel, packaged for TikTok (see `instagram-vs-tiktok.md`).
NOT a 1:1 repost. Faceless brand rule applies on TikTok too.

---

## Hook (0–1 sec) - voiceover, instant curiosity (no face)
> "Green flags that a city is safe enough for your first solo trip."

*(b-roll: calm walkable old town. No face, no men.)*

## Script (fast voiceover + on-screen captions over b-roll)
- "One - you can walk the whole centre on foot."
- "Two - people are still out after dark, cafés full."
- "Three - locals switch to English when you look lost."
- "Four - public transport is simple, no car needed."
- "Five - other solo women are already there. Save this."

## Spoken keywords (say them - TikTok is a search engine)
"solo female travel", "first solo trip", "safe cities for solo travel", "solo travel tips".

## On-Screen Captions
Auto-captions on (or Caption AI), plus big keyword call-outs. Karaoke style, active word highlighted.

## Caption (keyword-rich + invites a save)
> Green flags a city is safe to start solo 💚 Save this for your first trip.
> (TikTok, per §9: community tag + TikTok-only tags)
> #solofemaletravel #fyp #traveltok

## Sound
Trending travel / calm sound (check weekly). Voiceover over trending audio bed works well faceless.

## Reach levers after posting
- **Reply-with-video** to the top comment (e.g. "which city?") → second wave + feeds SOLO & SAFE.
- **Stitch invite:** "Stitch this with the city you felt safest in."

## Visual Production (faceless)
- B-roll only - streets, squares, cafés, transport, a woman from behind. **Kling AI / Higgsfield**
  for faceless scenes; **Caption AI** for captions. The `short-form-pipeline` can auto-cut + caption.
