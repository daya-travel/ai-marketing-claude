## Caption (full text - translatable, ends with save + send + 💚)
> The safest little capital in Europe - and almost nobody's first guess.
>
> Slovenia is one of the safest countries in the world, and its capital feels built for a
> first solo trip. Ljubljana's old town is car-free: just the river, a handful of bridges,
> and café tables in the sun. You can walk the whole centre end to end, and locals switch to
> English the moment you look a little lost.
>
> What to do:
> • Take the funicular up to Ljubljana Castle for rooftops on one side, the Alps on the other
> • Hop the free "Kavalir" electric buggy through the old town - locals use it, most tourists
>   never notice it
> • Day-trip to Lake Bled, 40 minutes by bus: the island church, a slow lake walk, back before dinner
>
> Save this for your first solo trip - and send it to the friend who keeps saying "one day". 💚
