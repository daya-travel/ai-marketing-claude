Du planst eine Solo-Reise nach Bali? Sieben Sachen, die dort wirklich verboten sind. Und Tourist:innen machen sie jeden Tag.

Eins. Vaping. Legal to own, fined in any smoke-free zone.

Zwei. Scooters. Your car permit doesn't count, and your insurance knows it.

Drei. Drones. Every one needs registering, even the tiny ones.

Vier. Cannabis. Same legal group as heroin. Possession is four years minimum.

Fünf. Small plastic bottles. Banned island-wide since January.

Sechs. Overstaying. One day over costs a million rupiah.

Sieben. Temples. The inner courtyards are closed to you, and photos get people deported.

Speicher dir das, bevor du fliegst.
