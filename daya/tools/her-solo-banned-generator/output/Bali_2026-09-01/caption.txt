Sieben Sachen, die in Bali wirklich verboten sind. Und Tourist:innen machen sie jeden Tag.

Vaping in the wrong place. Vapes are legal to own but count as smoking, and the smoke-free zones are wider than people expect. Import limits: 30 ml open-system or 12 ml closed-system e-liquid. Vaping in a smoke-free area is penalised like smoking.

The other one I'd watch is riding a scooter without the right permit. 300,000 to 1,000,000 rupiah, about $20 to $70, per missing document. And without a valid permit your insurer can refuse the hospital bill, which runs past $20,000 for a serious crash..

Every number here comes from Bali's own law, checked on 2026-09-01. Save it before you fly.
