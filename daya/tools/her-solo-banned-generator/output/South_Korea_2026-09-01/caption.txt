Seven things that are actually illegal in South Korea, and tourists do them every day.

Filming people without asking. It is a sexual offence here, not a privacy nuisance, and it applies in the street. Up to five years, seven if you post it, or 50 million won, about $36,500. It counts in public places too..

The other one I'd watch is vaping outside a smoking area. An instant 100,000 won fine, about $73, outside a marked smoking area..

Every number here comes from South Korea's own law, checked on 2026-09-01. Save it before you fly.
