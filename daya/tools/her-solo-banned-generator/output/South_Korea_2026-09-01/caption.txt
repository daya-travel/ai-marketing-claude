One thing in your carry-on is a narcotics charge in South Korea. Ten things worth checking before you fly.

A bad review. Truth is not a defence here. Saying something true about a person or a business can still be a crime. Up to three years even when it's true. Truth only counts as a defence if it served the public interest, and you have to prove that..

The other one I'd watch is undeclared food in your bag. Up to 10 million won, about $7,300, for not declaring it. Declare it and it is only confiscated, no fine..

Every number here comes from South Korea's own law, checked on 2026-09-01. Save it before you fly.
