One thing in this picture costs you a whole day. 3 things to sort before Japan.

Your suitcase. You don't have to drag it through a single station. Hand it in at the hotel desk and it's at the next hotel tomorrow. About 3,000 yen inside Tokyo, about $19, and it's there the next day.

Your bank card. Most Japanese bank ATMs simply don't take a card issued outside Japan. 7-Eleven has over 28,000 machines and they run all night.

The last train. The whole network stops overnight, and the taxi meter goes up while it's stopped. Taxis add 20 percent from 10pm, and there's no train to fall back on.

Checked on 2026-09-04, all of it from official pages and not from a blog. Save it for your Japan trip.
