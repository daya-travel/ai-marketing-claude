One thing in this picture gets you into the Vatican. 3 things to sort before you land.

A scarf. St Peter's and the Vatican Museums turn you away at the door, and it's a long way back to the hotel. A scarf over your shoulders is enough, and it lives in your bag.

A water bottle. Rome hands out cold drinking water on almost every corner, for nothing, all day and all night. Over 3,000 public fountains, running day and night.

The taxi from the airport. The price is fixed before you get in, so nobody can invent one for you at eleven at night. Fiumicino to the centre is a fixed 55 euros, about $64.

Checked on 2026-09-04, all of it from official pages and not from a blog. Save it for your Italy trip.
