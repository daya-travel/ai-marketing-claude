Vapes. Sold openly at home, so tourists pack one without thinking. Police in Bangkok and Phuket check for them specifically. Fines up to 30,000 baht, about $900. Bringing one through customs falls under the Customs Act..

The other one I'd watch is smoking on the beach. Up to a year in prison, or 100,000 baht, about $3,000..

Every number here comes from Thailand's own law, checked on 2026-09-01. Save it before you fly.
