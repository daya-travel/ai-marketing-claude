# Your First 24 Hours in a New City
### A calm, simple safety checklist for solo female travelers

*Honest, universal, and correct anywhere. No scary stuff - just the small moves that make the first day feel easy. Keep it on your phone.*

---

## Before you land
- [ ] Save the **local emergency number** and your **embassy address** offline (screenshot them).
- [ ] Share your **live location** and your **accommodation address** with one person you trust. Agree a **check-in time**.
- [ ] Screenshot the **route from the airport to your accommodation** (it works with no signal).
- [ ] Keep a little **local cash** for the first ride.
- [ ] Put one **SOS phrase in the local language** on your lockscreen (see the SOS Lockscreen Pack).

## The moment you arrive
- [ ] Use only **official or pre-booked transport** from the airport. Agree the price or the meter **before** you get in.
- [ ] Before the ride starts, send your **destination address and a photo of the car / plate** to your trusted person.
- [ ] At your accommodation: check the **door lock works**, find the **nearest exit**, and note the **address in the local language**.

## Your first walk
- [ ] Take a short walk **in daylight** around the block to get your bearings.
- [ ] Find the nearest **24h shop or pharmacy** and a **busy, well-lit street** - your safe points.
- [ ] If you need to check your map, step into a **shop or cafe**, not the middle of the street.

## Your first evening
- [ ] Keep your **phone charged** and carry a **power bank**.
- [ ] **Trust your gut.** If a place or a person feels off, leave. You owe no one an explanation.
- [ ] Tell your trusted person **"arrived, all good"** at your check-in time.

---

**The one rule that matters most:** prepared is not scared. You do not need to be fearless - you just need these small habits. Then the city is yours.
