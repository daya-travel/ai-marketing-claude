Your First 24 Hours in a New City

A calm, simple checklist for solo female travelers. Keep it on your phone.

Before you land

  • Save the local emergency number and your embassy address offline (screenshot them).
  • Share your live location and accommodation address with one trusted person. Agree a check-in time.
  • Screenshot the route from the airport to your accommodation (works with no signal).
  • Keep a little local cash for the first ride. Put an SOS phrase in the local language on your lockscreen.

The moment you arrive

  • Use only official or pre-booked transport. Agree the price or meter before you get in.
  • Send your destination and a photo of the car / plate to your trusted person before the ride starts.
  • At your accommodation: check the door lock works, find the nearest exit, note the address in the local language.

Your first walk

  • Take a short walk in daylight around the block to get your bearings.
  • Find the nearest 24h shop or pharmacy and a busy, well-lit street - your safe points.
  • To check your map, step into a shop or cafe, not the middle of the street.

Your first evening

  • Keep your phone charged and carry a power bank.
  • Trust your gut. If a place or person feels off, leave. You owe no one an explanation.
  • Send your trusted person "arrived, all good" at your check-in time.
The one rule that matters most: prepared is not scared. You do not need to be fearless - you just need these small habits. Then the city is yours.
